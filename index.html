Ioannis Papagiannis, PhD — EU Project Analyst

About Me

I am an EU Project Analyst at REZOS Brands, supporting the implementation of EU-funded R&D projects. My professional focus is on project implementation, coordination support and proposal development, building on a background in applied research and engineering.

I hold a PhD in Mechanical Engineering from Coventry University. My doctoral research included experimental collaboration with Imperial College London’s Hazelab, with Prof. Guillermo Rein as one of my PhD supervisors. My research and industrial R&D experience spans materials science, electrochemistry, hydrogen technologies and fire safety.

At REZOS, I support project documentation, reporting and the tracking of tasks, responsibilities, deadlines and resources. I am developing toward EU R&D project coordination through hands-on involvement in project implementation.
